## `01 / MISSION`

I am building toward a career in **AI Forward Deployed Engineering**.

My focus is to develop the engineering depth required to take a real-world problem from discovery and system design to implementation, integration, evaluation, and deployment.

I believe useful AI is not defined by a demo. It is defined by whether a system solves the right problem, works reliably, and creates measurable value.

> **Understand the Problem. Engineer the Solution. Deploy the Impact.**

## `04 / ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES`

- **Problem before technology:** understand the user's workflow before choosing a tool.
- **Fundamentals before frameworks:** learn how systems work, not just how to call libraries.
- **Build before claiming:** let working projects demonstrate capability.
- **Test before trusting:** validate behaviour, edge cases, and failure modes.
- **Own the outcome:** consider reliability, security, usability, and maintenance.
- **Communicate clearly:** explain technical decisions in language people can act on.
